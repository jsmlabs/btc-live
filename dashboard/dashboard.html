          Getting started / data and privacy

          Live: Validated Binance Futures market prices and the chart. Analytics: visible resting orders and executed taker trades. Neither predicts liquidations.

          Replay: Sample recordings and independent event recordings; imported sessions are unverified and never influence alerts. Saving a session requires explicit action.

          Alerts: Configure local targets and browser notifications. Diagnostics: inspect feed freshness and reconnects. No account, exchange API key or telemetry is required.

          Presets: Simple reduces available navigation; Advanced exposes all tools; Custom remembers your preferred landing view. Change the preset at any time.
